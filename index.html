About Nicky

The person behind the words

I'm Nicky — writer, mum, partner, and the neurotypical heart of a wonderfully neurodiverse family. My husband and son are both on the autism spectrum, and between us we've built a life that's full, complicated, hilarious, exhausting, and deeply loving.

For too long I felt like the voice of the NT parent or partner was missing from the conversation about autism. So I wrote the book I wished I'd had. And then I kept going.

Love, Parenting & Autism exists to give a proper home to the people who love someone on the spectrum — the ones who sometimes feel invisible in a world focused on the autistic experience. You matter too. Your feelings are valid. And you deserve support.

"I'm the only neurotypical person in my household. This isn't a clinical perspective — it's a lived one."
