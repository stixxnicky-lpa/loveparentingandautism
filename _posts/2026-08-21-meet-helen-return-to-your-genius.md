If you're reading this, there's a good chance you're stretched in about six directions at once. Kids. Maybe an aging parent. A career that doesn't pause just because your household has extra needs. And somewhere in there, you — the person actually holding it all together, usually last on your own list.

That's exactly the person Helen built her practice for. And I'm genuinely excited to introduce her to you.

![Helen Costa, Founder of Return to Your Genius](/assets/images/helen-costa-headshot.jpg)
*Helen Costa, Founder of Return to Your Genius*

## So, Who Is Helen?

Helen Costa is the Founder of **Return to Your Genius**, and honestly, the name says a lot before she even opens her mouth. Her mission is simple to say and hard to live without support: help you navigate the chaos of life so you can actually focus on what you do best — instead of running on fumes trying to do everything at once.

She works specifically with what's often called the "sandwich generation" — people over 35 who are trying to conceive or already raising children, while also caring for aging parents and holding down demanding careers. If any part of that sentence made you exhale a little too hard, you're her person.

## Where Her Expertise Comes From

Here's what stood out to me most: Helen's background isn't one lane, it's three, and they overlap in a really useful way. She's worked in **Pharma Research**, spent time in **International Teaching** (including with SEND learners), and has direct experience in **SEND Casework**.

That combination means she brings corporate-level precision — the kind you need when you're staring down a mountain of paperwork — together with genuine, lived empathy for what it actually feels like to advocate for a child, or for yourself, inside systems that weren't built with you in mind.

## What Working With Helen Actually Looks Like

Helen offers a few different ways to work together, depending on what you need right now:

**💬 The Coaching Space** — "Clarity Conversations" designed to help you reclaim your time and energy. Not vague pep talks — strategic, practical sessions.

**🔍 EHCP Clarity Audit** — If you've ever stared at an EHCP document feeling like it was written in a different language, this is for you. Helen does a specialist deep-dive into complex SEND paperwork so you're not carrying that weight alone.

**🇪🇸 Bilingual Support** — Helen speaks conversational Spanish and is expanding her practice to support business professionals and families beyond the UK, which feels particularly relevant to those of us building a life here in Spain.

Whether you need someone in your corner as a strategic partner, or a fierce advocate fighting alongside you for your family, Helen describes her role simply: she holds the map while you find your footing.

## Why I Wanted You to Meet Her

I built this community around one honest idea — that the neurotypical partner or parent in a neurodiverse household is doing real, exhausting, often invisible work too. Helen's whole practice is built around supporting exactly that overload: emotionally safe, practical, neuro-supportive spaces that help you reduce mental load, restore your capacity, and reconnect with yourself.

If you're in that sandwich — kids, parents, career, and barely any hours left for you — I'd really encourage you to look her up.
