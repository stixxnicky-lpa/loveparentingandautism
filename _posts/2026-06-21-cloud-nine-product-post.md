## Breaking Down the Features (The Good Stuff)

### Built-In Stress Ball Cuffs

This is the signature feature and the one that made me actually say "oh, come ON" out loud when I first saw it — in the best possible way.

The cuffs of the Cloud Nine hoodie contain a **built-in squeezable stress ball**. It's right there in the sleeve. You can squeeze it, press it, roll it between your fingers — all the proprioceptive input of a traditional stress ball, with none of the awkwardness of pulling one out of your pocket.

Research backs this up too: repetitive squeezing motions genuinely help reduce anxiety by giving the nervous system somewhere to send that energy. For autistic individuals who seek proprioceptive input — pressure, resistance, tactile feedback — this is the kind of low-key, always-available tool that can make the difference between a manageable day and a meltdown.

And for #sensoryprocessing parents who've watched their kid drop seventeen stress balls down the side of the sofa: the fact that it's *sewn into the sleeve* is a minor miracle.

### Chewable Silicone Drawstrings

The **Cloud Eleven** (their newest release) takes things further with **mouth-safe silicone drawstrings** — specifically designed for chewing.

Oral sensory seeking is incredibly common in autistic children and adults. It's one of those sensory behaviours that gets a lot of social judgement — kids get told off for chewing their collar, their pencil, their sleeves. Adults feel ashamed of a habit that their nervous system genuinely needs.

Cloud Nine's chewable drawstrings are made from food-grade silicone. They're safe, they're durable, and they're *built into a hoodie that looks like any other hoodie*. Your autistic teenager can wear this to school and nobody — not their classmates, not a judgy teacher — will know it doubles as a chew tool.

This one hit me right in the NT parent heart. Because I've watched the shame spiral that happens when a sensory need becomes the subject of someone else's commentary. Anything that removes that shame is a win. Full stop.

### Aromatherapy Scent Pocket

The **Aromatherapy Hoodie** has a discreet pocket designed to hold a scent pad — so calming essential oils stay close to the body all day, without anyone needing to know they're there.

Scent is one of the most powerful and immediate ways to calm the nervous system. It bypasses conscious thought and goes straight to the limbic system — the emotional processing centre of the brain. For sensory-sensitive individuals, a familiar calming scent can work as an anchor during moments of overwhelm.

Paired with the built-in stress ball cuff, you've got touch *and* scent working together simultaneously. Two sensory anchors in one discreet item of clothing.

For my NT self, this is also genuinely appealing — because navigating life in a neurodiverse household is its own kind of sensory and emotional marathon. I'm not above a little aromatherapy in my hoodie. I'm just saying.
