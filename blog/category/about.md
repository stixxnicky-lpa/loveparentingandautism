  <h1 style="font-family:'Playfair Display',serif;color:#3d1260;font-size:2.2rem;margin-bottom:8px;">About Nicky Stixx</h1>
  <p style="color:#6b6b8a;font-size:0.9rem;margin-bottom:40px;">Author · NT Wife · Mum · Lived-Experience Advocate</p>

  <p>I'm Nicky — the neurotypical half of a neurodiverse family for over 26 years. My husband and son are both on the autism spectrum, and everything I write comes from real, daily, lived experience — not clinical training, not a textbook, not a course.</p>

  <p>I wrote <strong><em>Love, Parenting & Autism</em></strong> because when I went looking for help, I couldn't find anything written <em>for me</em> — the NT partner, the NT parent, the person holding everything together while also trying to understand why life felt so hard. That gap is why this site exists.</p>

  <p>Here you'll find honest blog posts, practical PDF guides, and real resources — all created to support neurotypical people navigating neurodiverse family life. No jargon. No judgment. Just the stuff I wish someone had told me years ago.</p>
