What Makes It Different

Four sensory features. One hoodie. Nobody else knows.

Built-In Stress Ball Cuffs

A squeezable stress ball is sewn into the sleeve cuff — always available, completely invisible. Delivers proprioceptive input on demand, redirecting anxious energy without drawing a single glance. Research shows repetitive squeezing significantly reduces anxiety compared to having no outlet at all.

Chewable Silicone Drawstrings (Cloud Eleven)

Food-grade, mouth-safe silicone drawstrings meet oral sensory seeking needs discreetly. No more chewed collars, no stares, no being told off. A safe, durable, built-in outlet for a sensory need that's wildly common and wildly misunderstood.

A discreet internal pocket holds a scent pad all day. Calming essential oils trigger the limbic system's relaxation response — the brain's fastest path to calm — without anyone else needing to know they're there. Scent and touch working in tandem.
